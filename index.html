A quiet place to write

For the words
you still carry.

Write to a loved one who has passed away, someone you miss but can no longer speak with, or the person you used to be.

Some conversations stay with us.

There may be memories you want to revisit, words you never got to say, or something your past self needed to hear. Give them a little space on the page. Write at your own pace, without having to find a perfect ending.

A letter can remain a draft while you find your words. When you choose Send and burn, you can keep a readable copy to return to. The ritual can be a way to mark a moment, without having to make it a goodbye.
