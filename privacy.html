Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

ToHeaven is developed by yanhao wu. It is a personal writing space for letters to loved ones who have passed away, people you miss but can no longer speak with, or your past self. This policy explains how the current app and its related support pages handle information.

1. Information in the app

ToHeaven does not require an account. The current app does not operate a developer-controlled server for your letters and does not automatically transmit your writing or personal stamps to the developer.

The app stores the following locally:

  • Your current draft, including its recipient label, body, update time, selected stamp and wax seal, and Keep a copy choice.
  • Sent-letter records, including an internal identifier, recipient label, sending time, envelope decorations, stamp, wax seal, and copy status.
  • A letter’s body when you choose to keep a copy.
  • Personal photo stamps and their identifiers, creation dates, and removal status.
  • Preferences, including your default stamp and Sound setting.

These details allow the app to restore your draft, display Sent letters, preserve envelope decorations, and remember your choices. Recipient labels are not used to contact anyone. Local data remains until removed through the relevant app controls or until the app and its local data are deleted; backups are handled separately below.

2. Camera and photo selection

You may take a photograph or select an image to create a personal stamp. Camera access is requested when you use the camera feature. You can decline access and choose a photograph instead.

Photo selection uses Apple’s system picker. ToHeaven loads the image you select for cropping and previews the resulting stamp on your device.

When you confirm a crop, the app saves a newly rendered stamp image with its paper border. The current implementation does not save the full source photograph into its permanent stamp collection or automatically add camera photographs to your Photos library. Your existing source images remain governed by your Photos and iCloud settings.

A confirmed stamp is added to My stamps. Cancelling a later envelope selection does not remove a stamp you have already created.

3. Sending, burning and removal

Sending is a symbolic, local action. It does not transmit a letter to the named recipient.

If Keep a copy is enabled when you send, the body is stored separately from the envelope record. If it is disabled, the sent record is retained without a readable body, and the draft is removed.

Discarding a draft removes its saved content. The app may retain an internal identifier to prevent an interrupted or delayed save from restoring the discarded draft.

Confirming Burn copy deletes the saved body and marks the record as Copy burned. The recipient label, sending time, envelope, decorations, and stamp remain. The deletion is committed before the burning animation finishes.

Removing a personal stamp hides it from My stamps and clears applicable draft and default selections. If a sent letter still references that stamp, its image and record remain available for the historical envelope. Otherwise, the app cleans up the image and record.

4. Backups and security

App data is stored within the app’s local storage. Depending on your settings, Apple’s device, iCloud, or computer backup services may include that data. ToHeaven does not manage or erase those backups. Apple explains what iCloud Backup includes.

Deletion in ToHeaven does not guarantee removal from earlier backups, screenshots, source photographs, or copies outside the app. Restoring an earlier device backup may restore older app data. The burning effects do not certify secure erasure.

Protect your device with appropriate access controls. No storage method or security measure can guarantee complete protection.

5. Diagnostics and tracking

The current app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not automatically upload its diagnostic logs to the developer.

The app can produce technical system logs about rendering, audio, performance, and errors. Apple may process device diagnostics according to your system settings and Apple’s policies. If you choose to send diagnostic material to support, review it for private information first.

6. Support email and website visits

If you email BloatMonster@163.com, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you send. This information is used to respond, investigate issues, and handle related requests. Email delivery and storage also involve the relevant email providers.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to address the request, related follow-up, and applicable legal recordkeeping obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence, subject to those obligations. Please avoid sending letter text or personal photographs unless necessary.

This support website does not add advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. It has no contact form and does not use scripts to read or upload your app data.

When these pages are accessed through GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. That hosting activity is separate from the app’s local letter storage. See GitHub’s Pages documentation and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

7. Your choices and questions

Use the app’s discard, copy-burning, and personal-stamp removal features to manage the data they address. Camera permission and backup settings can be managed through your device.

The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete content stored only on your device. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to support, contact yanhao wu at BloatMonster@163.com.

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date. Material changes to data handling will be explained through an appropriate notice.
