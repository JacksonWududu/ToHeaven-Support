Common questions

Who can I write to?

ToHeaven is a place for letters to loved ones who have passed away, people you miss but can no longer speak with, or your past self. There is no required occasion or way to write. A letter can hold a memory, a thank-you, a question, or something you could not say at the time.

Does Send and burn deliver my letter?

No. It is a symbolic experience within ToHeaven. The app does not send physical mail or email, contact the recipient, or confirm delivery to another person.

Can I read a letter after sending it?

Enable Keep a copy before sending. Afterwards, open its envelope in Sent letters when it shows Copy saved. No copy and Copy burned records do not contain a readable body.

Can a discarded draft or burned copy be restored?

The app has no Undo or recovery service for these actions. The developer does not hold a server copy. Device backups are managed separately by Apple and your own backup settings. If burning reports a rendering problem after the copy was removed, retrying the animation does not restore the deleted body.

Why does an old envelope still show a stamp I removed?

Removing a personal stamp hides it from My stamps. Sent letters retain the stamp they originally used, so historical envelopes keep their appearance.

Can I make a stamp without camera access?

Yes. Choose a photo instead. You can manage camera permission in your device’s Settings.

How do I turn off sound?

Open ToHeaven’s Settings and turn Sound off. Your choice is saved across app restarts. Some system camera behavior can depend on your device and region.
