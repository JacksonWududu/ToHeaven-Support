3. Local storage and irreversible choices

The current app stores drafts, sent records, saved copies, personal stamps, and preferences locally. It does not provide a developer-operated account, cloud archive, synchronization service, or recovery service.

Before selecting Send and burn, decide whether to enable Keep a copy. If enabled, the app retains a readable local copy. If disabled, the app retains the sent record without a readable body.

Confirming the discard of a draft removes that draft. Confirming Burn copy removes the saved body while retaining its envelope record and associated details. These actions cannot be undone through the app. A burning animation represents the action visually; it is not a guarantee of forensic erasure from storage, backups, screenshots, or other copies.

Removing a personal stamp hides it from My stamps. A stamp used by a sent letter may remain stored so that the historical envelope can still display it.
